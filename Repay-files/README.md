# Repay

Research and ideas for the Sarvam AI x Cashfree buildathon: **agents that move real money** in Hindi, Tamil, Kannada and Bengali.

- Event: Sat 21 November 2026, 11:30 AM to 7 PM, Cashfree Office, Ecoworld, Bengaluru. 150 builders, 30 teams of 5. Rs 3,00,000+ in cash and credits. 500 Sarvam credits per registered builder.
- Brief: someone says what they want (voice, chat, documents or WhatsApp), the agent does it, the money moves, and the agent answers back.

## Ideas: 20 ideas, 4 themes (one file each)

| Theme | File | Ideas |
|---|---|---|
| Voice | [ideas/01-voice.md](ideas/01-voice.md) | Raitha Payout, Dihadi Desk, Ghar Bhejo, Saath-Saath Split, Doodh Hisaab |
| Chat | [ideas/02-chat.md](ideas/02-chat.md) | Salary Saathi, Bank-Badal Guard, SHG Hisaab, RWA Society Desk, Paisa Wapas Referee |
| Documents | [ideas/03-documents.md](ideas/03-documents.md) | Dadu Guard, Supplier Safe-Pay, Mandi Parchi, Tender EMD Desk, Muster Roll Reader |
| WhatsApp | [ideas/04-whatsapp.md](ideas/04-whatsapp.md) | Udhaar Vaani Loop, Tuition Fee Munshi, Puja Committee Kitty, PG Rent and Deposit Desk, Netting Desk |

Scores in the idea files are our own inference, not a sponsor rubric. Example sentences are romanised with an English gloss and need a native-speaker check. Everything runs on Cashfree and Sarvam sandbox or test keys only.
